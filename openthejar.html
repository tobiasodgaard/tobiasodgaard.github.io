Open the Jar

3D Indie Game Project, 2024 - ongoing

A storydriven point and click adventure game about a strange guy trying to open his pickle jar.

My main responsibility for the game is managing the production and making sure that everything is where it needs to be.

Role: Production Manager, Pipeline Manager, Modeler, Texturing Artist

Director: Federica Lazzari

Production Team: Adriana Navarro Villacampa, Alberto Allegri Rodríguez, Anastazja Konczakowska, Federica Lazzari, James F. Harlow, Matteo "Molle" Allegra, Rasmus Odgaard
