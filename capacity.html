Capacity of Spirit

3D Shortfilm Solo Project, 2024 - ongoing

In life's cycles of giving and taking, what is the capacity of a dead body?

Capacity of Spirit is a 3D short visualizing reflections on life and death. I wanted to explore what impact you leave behind and how material processes inform how we feel about it.

Role: The full concept and production is done solo.
