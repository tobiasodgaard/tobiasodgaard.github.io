Gltch Gathering

Trailer for Viborg Animation Festival, 2023

Viborg Animation Festival hits the town and the adrenaline rush of excitement is unbearable. Soon the whole town is in a riot of chaos and fit to burst.

"Gltch Gathering" is a 3D short developed as part of the educational programme at The Animation Workshop. The short has been used as a trailer for the festival in 2024. The short is a low poly movie and an homage to games of the early 2000s.

Role: Director, Compositor, Editor, Rigger

Director: Tobias Emil Odgaard

Production Team: Alex Dahlhus, Anton Skogsberg, Benjamin Otzen, Christian Væhrens, Daniel Meerson, Dominique Desanges, Elodie Ducas, Helena Rasmussen, Natalía Pantehis, Simon Bergendorff
